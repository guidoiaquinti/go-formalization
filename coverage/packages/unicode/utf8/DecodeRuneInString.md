Go returns ASCII directly and otherwise checks the lead byte, available width, and continuation byte ranges before assembling the payload. The Lean model uses the acceptance classifier to choose the width and combines the corresponding payload bits. Its theorems cover the empty and ASCII paths, a rejected non-ASCII lead, and accepted widths two through four conditional on the classifier. The differential comparison checks classifier agreement against an oracle that accumulates a scalar and rejects overlong forms, surrogates, and values above U+10FFFF. [Pinned Go source](https://github.com/golang/go/blob/go1.27.0/src/unicode/utf8/utf8.go#L219-L266) · [Lean model](../../../../lean/GoLean/Unicode/Utf8/DecodeRuneInString.lean)

On Go 1.27.0 darwin/arm64, the direct string call and Lean agreed with the independent oracle on 65,809 boundaries and 60,000 samples with seeds 17, 2718, and 65537. Boundaries include every singleton and two-byte string and 15 selected additional inputs. The two empty byte slices both convert to the empty Go string. The check compares both scalar and byte count.
