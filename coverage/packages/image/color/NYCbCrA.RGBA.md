Go uses the same fixed-point RGB conversion and saturation as YCbCr.RGBA, then multiplies those saturated channels by replicated alpha and divides by 65535. The theorem proves the products fit in uint32 and cancels the shared replication factor, yielding the floor of each saturated channel times the alpha byte divided by 255. The result alpha remains the replicated byte. The theorem covers every four-byte tuple. [Pinned source](https://github.com/golang/go/blob/go1.27.0/src/image/color/ycbcr.go#L247-L288)
