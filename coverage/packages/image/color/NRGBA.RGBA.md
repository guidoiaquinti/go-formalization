Go repeats each RGB byte, multiplies by the original alpha byte, and divides by 255 with truncation. Each product is at most 65535 times 255, within uint32. The theorem rewrites the result as p + floor(2p / 255), where p is the input byte times alpha. Alpha is widened by repeating its byte.
