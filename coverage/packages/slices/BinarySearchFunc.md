I checked Go 1.27.0's loop in `src/slices/sort.go` against the Lean interval search and its partition theorem. The unsigned midpoint equals the mathematical floor of the average of the two nonnegative bounds. At each probe a negative element-to-target comparison moves the lower bound past the probe. Otherwise the probe becomes the upper bound. The first nonnegative index, or the length if the suffix is empty, is the resulting index. The final flag checks both bounds and comparator equality to zero. The model covers pure, partitioned comparators and return values.
