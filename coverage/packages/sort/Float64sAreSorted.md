Go delegates to the reverse adjacent scan in `slices.IsSorted`. The float64 specialization of `cmp.Less` orders all NaNs before numbers, treats NaNs as equivalent to one another for ordering, and compares the remaining values in ascending order. The Lean theorem characterizes the absence of adjacent inversions using Float primitives. By transitivity, the Go oracle checks the same condition by examining every ordered pair. Correspondence of Go and Lean comparisons is tested on binary64 bit patterns.
