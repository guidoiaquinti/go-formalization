The pinned comparator uses ordinary strict comparison and orders a NaN before a non-NaN. It treats two NaNs as equivalent for ordering, and neither signed zero precedes the other. Lean models this formula through its Float primitives and proves the returned Boolean or first invalid index. Cross-runtime behavior of those primitives is covered by the Go comparison over raw bit patterns. The proof abstracts the runtime panic object, while the driver checks the recovered bounds panic text.
