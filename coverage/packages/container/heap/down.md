The pinned helper checks the left-child bound and signed overflow guard before calling the comparator. A right child, when present, is compared with the left before the selected child is compared with the parent. The result records whether any swap moved the original index downward. For nonnegative Go `int` indices, overflowing `2*i+1` is negative and exits before callbacks. A valid left child is below the representable length, so computing its right sibling cannot overflow. The natural-index model rejects the same overflowing cases as out of range. Lean proves the leaf outcome, a sole-left stop, and the right-child swap step.
