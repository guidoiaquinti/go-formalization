Go 1.27.0 puts the high and low words into the first and second eight-byte ranges in big-endian order. The Lean theorem expresses each of the sixteen output positions as a base-256 digit of the corresponding word. The receiver abstraction takes the words directly because Go reads only those fields. The Go zero value supplies zero words, and the IPv4 constructor supplies the IPv4-mapped prefix. IPv6 zones affect the detail handle.
