Go replaces the detail handle with the native IPv4 handle only when the receiver is IPv4-mapped IPv6. The Lean theorem expresses that condition with the exact mapped prefix. The operation keeps the raw words in both cases. Conversion clears the zone and changes the family width to 32. Every other receiver keeps its original family and zone. The claim abstracts handle identity. [Pinned source](https://github.com/golang/go/blob/go1.27.0/src/net/netip/netip.go#L477-L486) · [Lean model](../../../../lean/GoLean/Net/Netip/AddrUnmap.lean)

Go 1.27.0 and Lean agreed on 101 boundaries and 60,000 samples on darwin/arm64, using seeds 17, 2718 and 65537. The boundary set checks a one-bit change at every position of the mapped prefix. Go passed six independent fixtures. Returning all addresses unchanged failed two fixtures, and ignoring one mapped-prefix bit misclassified the near-prefix case.
