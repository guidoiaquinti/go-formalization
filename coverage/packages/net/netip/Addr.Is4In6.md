I checked Go 1.27.0's `Addr.Is4In6` against `addrIs4In6` and its theorem. The pinned method requires an IPv6 detail handle and a zero high word, then compares the high 32 bits of the low word with `0xffff`. The theorem expresses the shift as division by `2^32` and leaves the final 32 bits unconstrained. Zone contents have no effect on the detail-class test.

I ran `go run difftest/net/netip/addr-is4in6/check.go` on Go 1.27.0, darwin/arm64. The native predicate, Lean result and 12-byte prefix oracle agreed on 100 boundaries and 20,000 cases each for seeds 17, 2718 and 65537.
