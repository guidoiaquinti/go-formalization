Go checks zero first, then overflow. It divides directly when the high word is zero and otherwise performs normalized long division. The model abstracts the long-division steps. The theorem checks the two panic conditions and reconstructs the double-width dividend as divisor times quotient plus remainder, with remainder strictly below divisor. These conditions uniquely determine the observable result. [Pinned source](https://github.com/golang/go/blob/go1.27.0/src/math/bits/bits.go#L514-L568) · [Lean model](../../../../lean/GoLean/Math/Bits/Division.lean)

On Go 1.27.0 darwin/arm64, Lean and the arbitrary-precision Go oracle agreed on 1,728 boundary triples and 60,000 sampled triples per profile, using seeds 17, 2718, and 65537. Both profiles check exact quotient and remainder words. The panic comparison requires a runtime.Error with the corresponding exact message, either `runtime error: integer divide by zero` or `runtime error: integer overflow`. The fallback disables compiler intrinsics in the driver and math/bits package. [Differential comparison](../../../../difftest/math/bits/div64/check.go)
