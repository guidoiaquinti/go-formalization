Go dispatches on the native uint width. The model and comparison cover its 64-bit branch, which delegates to RotateLeft64. That helper masks the converted signed count to obtain the Euclidean residue and combines a left shift with the wrapped bits from a right shift. Lean proves the exact source position of each output bit. The 32-bit uint wrapper and timing are outside the claim. [Pinned Go source](https://github.com/golang/go/blob/go1.27.0/src/math/bits/bits.go#L173-L181) · [Lean model](../../../../lean/GoLean/Math/Bits/Rotation.lean)

Go 1.27.0 and Lean agreed on 1,596 boundaries and 60,000 samples per profile on darwin/arm64, with seeds 17, 2718 and 65537. The oracle places individual input bits at their rotated positions. The second profile disables compiler intrinsics in the driver and math/bits package. [Differential comparison](../../../../difftest/math/bits/rotateleft/check.go)

Go passed nine independent fixtures for this width in both profiles. Replacing rotation with a shift failed three cases. Rotating in the opposite direction failed four, and taking the magnitude of a negative count failed two. The fixtures include both signed int extrema and multiples of the width.
