Go computes the low word in uint64 and derives carry from the operand and result bits. Lean proves that the low word plus the radix multiplied by the output carry equals the exact sum, including the input carry. The low word lies below the radix and the output flag is Boolean, so the equation determines both outputs. The input carry is restricted to 0 or 1 by Go's contract. Timing is outside the claim.
