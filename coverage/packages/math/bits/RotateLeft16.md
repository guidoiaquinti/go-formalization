Go masks the signed count after converting it to uint. For width 16, the low bits of that conversion give the Euclidean remainder, even for negative counts and MinInt. The source combines a truncated left shift and a right shift supplying the wrapped bits. Lean proves the exact source position of each output bit. A zero residue leaves the input unchanged. Timing is outside the claim. [Pinned Go source](https://github.com/golang/go/blob/go1.27.0/src/math/bits/bits.go#L194-L201) · [Lean model](../../../../lean/GoLean/Math/Bits/Rotation.lean)

Go 1.27.0 and Lean agreed on 444 boundaries and 60,000 samples per profile on darwin/arm64, with seeds 17, 2718 and 65537. The oracle places individual input bits at their rotated positions. The second profile disables compiler intrinsics in the driver and math/bits package. [Differential comparison](../../../../difftest/math/bits/rotateleft16/check.go)

Go passed nine independent fixtures for this width in both profiles. Replacing rotation with a shift failed three cases. Rotating in the opposite direction failed four, and taking the magnitude of a negative count failed two. The fixtures include both signed int extrema and multiples of the width.
