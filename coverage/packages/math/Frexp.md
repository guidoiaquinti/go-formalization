The Go source preserves signed zeros, infinities and NaN words and returns exponent zero for them. Otherwise it normalizes the input, sets the fraction’s exponent field to 1022, and adjusts the returned exponent. Multiplication by 2^52 on a nonzero subnormal is exact. If its top payload bit is at position k, normalization moves that bit into the implicit leading position and shifts the remaining bits into the fraction field. The Lean theorem expresses the same result using the original fields and k. [Pinned Go source](https://github.com/golang/go/blob/go1.27.0/src/math/frexp.go#L17-L39) · [Pinned normalization](https://github.com/golang/go/blob/go1.27.0/src/math/bits.go#L59-L67) · [Lean model](../../../lean/GoLean/Math/Frexp.lean)

Go and Lean compare both the exact returned word and integer exponent against independent field arithmetic, including NaN signs and payloads. Go 1.27.0 on darwin/arm64 agreed on 336 boundaries and 60,000 samples under each profile. The samples use seeds 17, 2718 and 65537. The second profile disables compiler intrinsics in the driver and math package. The native wrapper selects the Go implementation on this platform.
