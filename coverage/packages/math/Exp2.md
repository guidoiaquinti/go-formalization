I checked the pinned Go wrapper and generic reduction in src/math/exp.go and the arm64 implementation in src/math/exp_arm64.s. Both preserve NaN words and select the same overflow and underflow cutoffs. The model retains the rounded integer reduction, split ln(2) constants, polynomial and rational correction, and scaling. Arm64 fuses the polynomial additions and final multiply-subtract and stages subnormal scaling through a 52-bit exponent adjustment. The inspected amd64 generic disassembly uses separate operations in v1 and fused polynomial additions in v3, leaving the final multiply and subtract separate. The kernel theorems establish the cutoffs, exact NaN word preservation and both signed zero inputs returning one. Finite rounding correspondence is differential.
