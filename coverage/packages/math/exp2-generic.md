I checked private exp2 and expmulti in Go 1.27.0 src/math/exp.go. The source preserves NaN words and checks its overflow and underflow cutoffs before reduction. It splits the ln(2) multiplier, evaluates the polynomial and rational correction, then scales using Ldexp. The pinned arm64 compiler output for expmulti has four FMADDD instructions and one FMSUBD. The inspected generic amd64 v1 output uses separate rounding, and v3 fuses the polynomial additions while keeping the final subtraction separate. The extended Lean profile models generic arm64's contraction while retaining generic Ldexp scaling. Public profiles still use their previous rounding and scaling paths. The Lean proofs cover exact NaN words, cutoffs and the two signed zeros. Finite rounding correspondence is differential.
