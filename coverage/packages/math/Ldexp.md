The Go denormal branch stages the exponent by 53 and multiplies by 2^-53. The Lean theorem combines the wrapped additions, reconstructs the coefficient from the original fields, and gives the exact rounding of the denormal coefficient with ties to even. ## Go-only contract check

The retrospective probe checks 52 deterministic pairs of signed halves and twos with exponents around the binary64 rounding boundaries and native integer extrema. The oracle scales the exact dyadic with `math/big.Float` and rounds to binary64. On Go 1.27.0 darwin/arm64, signed halves at `MinInt` returned signed infinity instead of signed zero, and signed twos at `MaxInt` returned signed zero instead of signed infinity. The public contract at these extrema remains a potential finding. Lean proves the four observed words for both 32- and 64-bit model widths. Native 32-bit execution was outside this run.
