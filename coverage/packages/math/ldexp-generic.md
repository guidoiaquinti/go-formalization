I checked Go 1.27.0's private helper in `src/math/ldexp.go`. Signed zeros, infinities, and NaNs return unchanged before normalization. Other inputs normalize subnormals exactly and add the normalization adjustment and unbiased exponent in native signed int arithmetic. Combining the two additions in the model is valid under arithmetic modulo the native word size. Thresholds apply to the wrapped exponent. In the denormal path Go temporarily raises the exponent by 53 and multiplies by (2^{-53}), which rounds the exact dyadic magnitude to the nearest representable word, breaking ties toward an even low bit. The Lean theorem specifies the rounded coefficient and sign. The unrestricted mathematical scaling law and its counterexamples are recorded separately in FINDINGS.md.
