I compared the pinned Go 1.27.0 generic implementation with the Lean word model. It preserves signed zeros, rejects magnitudes outside the domain by NaN classification, and evaluates the two arctangent reductions on either side of 0.7. The model distinguishes the arm64 fused radicand from the separate amd64 multiplication and subtraction. It also preserves the compiler's contraction choices in the shared arctangent model. Lean proves signed-zero preservation, out-of-domain classification and odd reflection in range.

On darwin/arm64 the comparison matched exact results on 78 boundaries and 60,000 sampled binary64 words with seeds 17, 2718 and 65537. The independent 256-bit reference differed by at most two binary64 encoding steps. Native amd64 and the s390x override remain unverified locally.
