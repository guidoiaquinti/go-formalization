The private floor helper returns signed zeros, infinities and NaNs before running Modf. For negative finite inputs with a nonzero fraction it increases the integer magnitude by one before restoring the sign. The Lean theorem verifies the corresponding encoded truncation and conditional unit increment, including carries into the next exponent. The theorem concerns the binary64 word formula. The mathematical floor relationship is checked independently with exact rational arithmetic in the Go comparison. [Pinned source](https://github.com/golang/go/blob/go1.27.0/src/math/floor.go#L21-L33)

Go 1.27.0 and Lean agreed on 94 boundary words and 60,000 samples with seeds 17, 2718, and 65537 on darwin/arm64. The same-package fixture invokes the private helper directly. It compares exact non-NaN words and compares NaNs by class. Run `go run difftest/math/floor-ceil/check.go` after building the registered Lean driver.
