Unsigned wrap makes zero take the unchanged path. The guard also returns unchanged magnitudes at least 2^52, including infinities and NaNs. The remaining magnitudes convert exactly to a truncated int64 and back to binary64. The assembly subtracts one when the original value is less than that truncation. The proved exponent and discarded-bit formula represents this result and preserves signed zero. The review observes the NaN class and abstracts payloads and exception flags. It covers the arm64 and amd64 implementations in the registered scope.
