I checked the pinned Go 1.27.0 generic implementation against the Lean model. The model preserves the order of the signed-zero, infinity and NaN cases and computes the ordinary quadrant from the rounded quotient. For lower-left inputs whose quotient underflows to positive zero, the source selects positive π. Lean proves the NaN case, signed-zero behavior for a zero ordinate, and the underflow branch.

On darwin/arm64 the direct comparison matched exact results on 1,302 boundaries and 60,000 samples using seeds 17, 2718 and 65537. The 256-bit independent reference was within one binary64 encoding step outside the 3,533 observed lower-left zero-rounding cases. In those cases the pinned implementation and model return positive π and the mathematical reference rounds to negative π. Native amd64 and the s390x override remain unverified locally. [Potential finding](../../../FINDINGS.md#potential--mathatan2-selects-positive-π-after-lower-left-quotient-underflow)

The independent control passed 20 native Go fixtures and 40 Lean profile rows. It checks both adjacent representable values of the abscissa at −2, the signed-zero quadrant and the infinity cases. Removing the sign fails nine fixtures. Substituting the mathematical quadrant for the observed underflow branch fails two fixtures.
