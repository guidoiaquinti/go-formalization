I checked the pinned public wrapper and generic helper in src/math/remainder.go. The supported native profiles select the generic path. After handling special values and equal magnitudes, the helper reduces modulo twice the divisor when that doubling fits. A larger divisor already has twice its value above every finite dividend. The tiny branch doubles the residual for the half comparison and avoids rounding a subnormal half. Above that threshold the divisor's coefficient in smallest-subnormal units is even, so halving is exact. The first centering test is strict and the second is inclusive. Together they select an even quotient at a tie.

The Lean model retains those branches and uses exact dyadic units. Its kernel theorem covers unequal finite magnitudes under the positive-divisor, exact-half and safe-period conditions described above. The full comparison also covers equal magnitudes, signed zeros, infinities, NaNs and conversion back to binary64. NaNs are observed by class.
