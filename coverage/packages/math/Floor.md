The generic source returns zeros, infinities and NaNs through an early branch. It uses the integer part from `Modf` and adjusts negative inputs only when their fractional part is nonzero. `Modf` gets the integer part from `Trunc` and subtracts that part from the input. That subtraction is exact on finite magnitudes. It is a subtraction from zero or between operands within a factor of two. In the Lean model clearing the fractional bits gives the integer part, and their residue identifies the adjustment. For negative magnitudes below one the adjustment yields negative one. For larger fractional magnitudes the encoded unit increment advances past the truncated integer and carries into the exponent if needed. [Pinned Floor source](https://github.com/golang/go/blob/go1.27.0/src/math/floor.go#L7-L34) · [Pinned Modf source](https://github.com/golang/go/blob/go1.27.0/src/math/modf.go#L7-L18) · [Lean model](../../../lean/GoLean/Math/FloorCeil.lean)

An exact rational quotient and remainder independently checks the result. Go 1.27.0 and Lean agreed on 94 boundaries and 60,000 samples per profile on darwin/arm64, with seeds 17, 2718 and 65537. The runs call public `math.Floor` normally and with compiler intrinsics disabled in the driver and math package. On this platform the wrapper can still select assembly. Each run also checks the private `floor` function via a test overlay compiled with its own normal settings. Native NaN results are compared by class.
