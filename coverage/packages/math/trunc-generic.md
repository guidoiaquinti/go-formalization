For unbiased exponents below 52 the mask clears exactly the bits strictly below the unit position. Larger exponents, infinity and NaN permit the original word to pass through. The Lean theorem proves that the mask equals clearing the remainder for the appropriate power of two, including unchanged encodings for all exponent-2047 inputs.
