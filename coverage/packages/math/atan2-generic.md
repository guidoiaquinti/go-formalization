I checked the Go 1.27.0 private entry point against the shared Lean model. The pinned code preserves signed-zero and infinity quadrants through ordered special cases. For finite nonzero inputs, it selects the final quadrant using the arctangent of the rounded quotient. Lean preserves the resulting positive π for lower-left inputs whose quotient rounds to positive zero.

The same-package overlay matched exact results on 1,302 boundaries and 60,000 samples with seeds 17, 2718 and 65537 on darwin/arm64. The independent numerical reference differed by at most one binary64 encoding step outside 3,533 lower-left zero-rounding inputs. In those inputs Go and Lean returned positive π while the reference rounded to negative π. Native amd64 remains unverified locally. [Potential finding](../../../FINDINGS.md#potential--mathatan2-selects-positive-π-after-lower-left-quotient-underflow)

The independent control passed 20 direct private Go fixtures and 40 Lean profile checks. It exercises the underflow tie at an ordinate of `-2 * math.SmallestNonzeroFloat64` and an abscissa of `-4`, both neighbors of that abscissa, and negative infinity. Losing the result sign fails ten fixtures. Selecting the mathematical quadrant in the underflow branch fails two.
