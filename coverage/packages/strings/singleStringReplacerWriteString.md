The pinned method chooses the writer interface once. It writes an unmatched span and the replacement for each nonoverlapping match, then writes the final tail. It attempts empty payloads, includes each returned count, and stops on the first nonnil error after adding that call's count. A short write with nil error does not stop the method. The model covers constructor-produced receivers with nonempty patterns and deterministic writer replies with counts bounded by the payload. Panic, nonterminating writers, and integer overflow are outside scope. Lean proves the no-match trace and two concrete cases, an immediate error on the empty leading span and continuation after a short nil-error reply.
