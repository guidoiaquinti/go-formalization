Go checks whether the cursor is at the start before checking the previous-rune marker. Either error returns without changing the state. Success rewinds to the marked byte offset and clears the marker. The Lean theorems specify those cases for nonnegative cursor states and represent the nil panic separately. The independent control checks both ordinary errors, then reads and rewinds a multibyte rune at a positive offset. The next UnreadRune fails because the marker was cleared, and the next ReadRune recovers the entire rune. A mutant rewinding one byte instead starts at the continuation byte and is rejected.
