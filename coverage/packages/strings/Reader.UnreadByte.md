The pinned method checks the cursor before either field assignment. At zero it returns the beginning-of-string error and preserves the state. A positive cursor decrements by one and resets the previous-rune marker even beyond the end of the string. The Lean theorem covers these branches on nonnegative cursor states and represents nil receiver panic separately.
