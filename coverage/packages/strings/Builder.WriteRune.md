Go checks the receiver identity before calculating the old buffer length or encoding the rune. An unset identity becomes the receiver pointer. A different initialized identity panics with the copied-builder message. A nil receiver panics on the field access. Successful calls delegate to utf8.AppendRune, add its bytes, return the exact increase in length and return nil error. The Lean theorems prove the bytes and identity transition, invalid-rune replacement, and failure outcomes. Allocation sizing and allocation failures are outside the model.
