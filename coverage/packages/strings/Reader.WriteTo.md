The pinned method clears the rune marker first and skips the writer at EOF. Otherwise it calls io.WriteString once on the unread suffix, rejects counts larger than that suffix, and advances by the count before returning the writer's error or synthesizing a short-write error. A negative count takes the signed cursor backwards. The Lean theorems cover these branches and the nil receiver and writer cases. The modeled callback leaves the reader alone.
