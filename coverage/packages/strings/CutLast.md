`CutLast` uses the greatest matching byte offset returned by `LastIndex`. The Lean scan checks each byte window in descending order. The pinned Rabin–Karp implementation uses a rolling hash and verifies equality before accepting a candidate. Empty and single-byte separators and equal or larger lengths have dedicated branches in Go. The model captures their results through the empty-separator case and byte-window comparisons. The two Lean theorems establish maximality and the exact split, including the unchanged input and false flag on a miss. The review covers byte contents and offsets. Backing-storage identity and the hash arithmetic are outside the claim.
