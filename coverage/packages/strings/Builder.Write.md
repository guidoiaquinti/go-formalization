Go checks receiver identity before appending the input bytes. A zero receiver establishes identity even for a nil or empty write. A copied, identified receiver panics before changing bytes, and a nil receiver panics. The Lean theorems cover these outcomes alongside the successful write count and resulting bytes. Allocation and post-append capacity are outside scope.
