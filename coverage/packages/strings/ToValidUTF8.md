Go first locates the first malformed byte and starts a builder there, copying the valid prefix. In the scoped raw-output model, that prefix is emitted unchanged by the shared scanner. The scanner emits the replacement's exact raw bytes once per contiguous malformed run and resets at the next valid rune. Go's fast-path identity and builder allocation details are outside the byte-result claim. Lean proves ASCII preservation, one replacement for a malformed run, and preservation of a literal RuneError between malformed runs.
