Go checks receiver identity before validating the request. A negative request on a zero builder leaves its identity established. If there is enough remaining capacity, Go retains it. Otherwise Go requests an allocation of twice the previous capacity plus the requested additional space, copies the existing bytes, and preserves the length. Lean proves these paths with an allocator parameter. Allocation failure, arithmetic overflow, and backing pointer identity are outside scope.
