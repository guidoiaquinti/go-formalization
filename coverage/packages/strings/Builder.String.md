The comparison includes zero, initialized, and nil receivers, every singleton byte, and scripts with growth and reset. [Go comparison](../../../difftest/strings/builder-string/check.go)

The independent control checks the empty result after reservation, stable storage across two calls on a nonempty builder, and preservation of an earlier string through allocation and reset.
