The pinned helper compares bytes from the right and stops at the first mismatch or when either input ends. The Lean theorem characterizes every candidate length at or below the result by both input bounds and equality of their last bytes at that length. It therefore characterizes the maximal matching suffix. The scope includes raw bytes and suffixes that start inside UTF-8 encodings.
