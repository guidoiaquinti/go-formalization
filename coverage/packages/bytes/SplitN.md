The pinned wrapper delegates to the byte splitter. Zero returns nil, and a positive limit can leave an unsplit suffix with spare capacity. With an empty separator, the helper splits at decoded rune widths, preserving the original malformed bytes. A final slice created by decoding is capped at its length, while a raw suffix returned at the limit retains capacity. The Lean theorems cover the zero- and one-limit cases.
