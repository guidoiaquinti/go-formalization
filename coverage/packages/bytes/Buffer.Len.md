In reachable states this equals the unread suffix length, with zero when the offset reaches the end. Accessing the buffer fields through a nil receiver panics. Lean states those three results, and its executor accepts geometries whose offsets lie within the stored length.

The direct comparison passed 17,161 boundary cases and 60,000 cases from seeds 17, 2718 and 65537. Its setup uses a history-derived geometry whose subtraction is preserved when Go reclaims or moves backing storage.
