Pinned Go 1.27.0 bytes/bytes.go ranges over the cutset string, comparing
the decoded rune at each step with the signed input and returning at the
first match. The Lean model reuses the reviewed raw-byte string decoder,
consumes its width at each step, and preserves the recursive match rule.
Its termination removes at least the leading byte. Theorems cover empty
input, a decoding step and its recurrence, and rejection of negative
targets. Malformed UTF-8 yields RuneError with width one, while a literal
encoding can also match RuneError.

The author checks 11,302 deterministic inputs and 60,000 signed-rune
samples using seeds 17, 2718 and 65537, with an explicit UTF-8 decoding
loop as reference. The overlay calls the original private declaration
and bypasses the public bytes wrapper. The independent control exercised
19 fixed private Go and Lean comparisons on darwin/arm64, including
partial UTF-8, invalid continuation bytes, overlong and surrogate
encodings, literal RuneError, signed extremes, and a valid match after
a malformed byte. Replacing traversal with an encoded-target search
failed nine cases. Deleting malformed input before traversal failed four.
