The model preserves the empty cutset, singleton input, singleton cutset,
ASCII table threshold and forward decoding paths in Go 1.27.0. An ASCII
byte cannot occur as a UTF-8 continuation, so raw-byte membership for
ASCII agrees with decoded membership. A non-ASCII singleton byte
decodes as RuneError on either side. The model checks all other
members against decoded runes and accumulates original byte widths.
The source's width-equality comparison and architecture-specific
encoded substring probes preserve that membership for valid non-ASCII
runes. Those optimizations and their probe orders remain outside scope.

The author comparison uses forward range decoding over both byte inputs
and first byte offset, with 69,856 boundaries and 60,000 samples using
seeds 17, 2718 and 65537. The independent control checked 20 native
darwin/arm64 and Lean fixtures. They cover malformed versus literal
RuneError, the singleton priorities, UTF-8 continuation lookalikes,
distinct potential matches and the 8/9 ASCII table transition. Raw-byte
membership, returning the rune ordinal, and selecting the last match
failed 8, 5 and 9 cases respectively.
