A nil receiver panics for every key. The Lean model proves the lookup and nil outcome and represents the complete table extensionally.

The pinned comparison covers every key on 514 boundary tables, 240 sampled tables and a nil receiver, for 193,280 lookups. The random seeds are 17, 2718 and 65537. The bridge constructs the table from explicit expected Boolean slots, and native Go and Lean each compare every result with its corresponding slot or nil-pointer panic.

The independent control checks all 256 keys on five fixed tables and nil, for 1,536 observations. Masking off the high bit fails five lookups, advancing the index by one fails 270, and replacing the panic with false fails all 256 nil-receiver lookups. The tables include transitions at every encoded word boundary and at the end of the byte range.
