Go 1.27.0 reports whether bytes.IndexAny returned a nonnegative byte
index. The Lean wrapper tests whether its modeled search returned an
offset, preserving offset zero and empty operands. The shared model
uses decoded rune membership for Unicode and accounts for literal
RuneError and malformed bytes. Architecture-specific Unicode probes
are outside the timing and algorithmic scope.

The author comparison uses independent Go range decoding and checks
69,856 boundary inputs and 60,000 samples with seeds 17, 2718 and 65537.
The independent control passed 18 fixed darwin/arm64 Go and Lean
fixtures covering empty operands, singleton malformed and literal
replacement bytes, continuation-byte lookalikes, ASCII gate boundaries
and matches at offset zero and later. Raw-byte membership and treating
offset zero as a miss failed nine and five cases respectively.
